Example

Example is a generative engine optimization platform. It tracks whether answer engines mention a brand, then helps the team publish pages those engines can quote.

The product covers citation monitoring, content atomization, Schema.org JSON-LD, and llms.txt. Teams use it to close the loop between a missing citation and a page that is easier to quote.
